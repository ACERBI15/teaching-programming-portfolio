About This Portfolio

I created this website for EDS124BR: Teaching Computational Thinking for Everyone. It documents what I learn throughout the course and shows how my understanding of teaching programming develops over time.

As a Computer Science student, I am familiar with learning technical ideas from a student’s perspective. In this course, I want to look beyond simply making code work. I want to learn how to explain difficult concepts in a simple way, design activities that keep learners involved, and make computing feel approachable for people with different levels of experience.
